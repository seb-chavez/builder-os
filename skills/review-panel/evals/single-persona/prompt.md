# Proposal: Charge patients a no-show fee through Tidewell

## Problem
No-shows cost dental practices revenue every week.

## Proposal
Require a card on file at booking and automatically charge a 50 dollar fee when a patient misses an appointment.

## Evidence
One practice owner we spoke to said this would cut no-shows in half.

## Plan
Launch to all practices in six weeks. Practices will turn it on because it pays for itself.
