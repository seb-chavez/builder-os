# Project brief: Waitlist auto-fill

## Problem
When a Tidewell patient cancels, the front desk calls the waitlist by hand. Most freed slots go unfilled.

## Proposal
Text the next three waitlisted patients when a slot opens. First to reply YES gets the slot.

## Success
Fill 40% of cancelled slots within the first quarter.
